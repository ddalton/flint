  • Consistency 4 — the hub is the one coherence authority: real byte-range locks, close-to-open, atomic rename across every client; acked writes are crash-durable on the PVC. Docked for the write-back RPO to S3 — there is no fsync-to-S3 verb until strict mode ships.
  • Security 1.5 vs 3.5 — AUTH_SYS carries no credential: reachability is the boundary, no root-squash, and nfsClientCIDRs is vacuous cross-cluster (1486/1486 connections SNAT'd). krb5p adds proven per-user identity + wire privacy, but leaves prefix-reuse adoption (B12), the root hub container, and pre-auth DoS untouched.
  • Day-2 2.5 vs 1.5 — one hub is a SPOF: node loss means D-state hangs and a ~6-min force-detach. Kerberos adds a KDC, per-node keytabs and rpc.gssd on every client — and the in-tree GSS code is dormant, not wired.
  • Consistency 2.5 — per-subtree RPO-consistent snapshots, one writer per subtree; fsync means the pod's emptyDir, never S3; byte-range locks, shared mmap and cross-subtree rename are refused (ENOLCK / EXDEV).
  • Security 3 — every pod holds prefix-scoped bucket credentials: narrower cross-tenant reach than AUTH_SYS, but a compromised pod owns its subtree's .flint/ cells, and the privileged /dev/fuse sidecar conflicts with PSA baseline/restricted.
  • Day-2 2 — nothing standing per share, but loss becomes a per-pod statistic: drain wiring, restart-in-place choreography, an 80–110 s lease lockout on unclean death — and the correctness core is unbuilt (track deprioritized in favor of lean).
  • Consistency 2 — same snapshot contract as FUSE with lower capture fidelity (mtime/size scan, torn-hot-file residual, no O_EXCL primitive) — though the 7-step barrier, inbox and merge manifest are built and TLA+-modeled.
  • Security 4 — pods hold zero bucket write credentials; the bucket trusts one principal again. The gateway turns detection into refusal, revocation returns to seconds, and the sidecar is an ordinary unprivileged process — no PSA conflict.
  • Day-2 3.5 — idle is S3-only structurally, the gateway is stateless N-replica; but gateway/proxy down wedges checkouts, restarts, sync and HITL writes, and publish amplification is real (a hot 2 GiB file ≈ 2.9 TiB/day at 60 s cadence).
