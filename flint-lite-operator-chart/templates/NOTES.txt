Watching: {{ .Values.watchNamespace | default "all namespaces" }}

Create a share:

  kubectl apply -f - <<'EOF'
  apiVersion: flint.io/v1alpha1
  kind: FlintShare
  metadata:
    name: tenant-a
    namespace: {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  spec:
    persistence:
      size: 20Gi
    # Tier to a bucket (optional — without it the PVC is the only copy
    # of the data). The bucket must already exist, with VERSIONING ON.
    # bucket: my-team-flint
    # keyPrefix: tenant-a/          # must end with "/"; immutable
    # credentialsSecretRef: flint-s3   # "" = IRSA / instance role
  EOF

Watch it come up (a tiered share is legitimately "Starting" for
minutes — it claims the volume epoch, which may wait out a dead
holder's lease, and may import the whole bucket, all before its
listener binds):

  kubectl get flintshares -A -w
  kubectl describe flintshare tenant-a -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

Mount it from any pod in the cluster:

  <address from `kubectl get fsh`>  e.g. tenant-a.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:/data/exports

Two things worth knowing:

  * Deleting a FlintShare keeps its PVC (reclaim: Retain by default)
    and NEVER touches the bucket.
  * At most one share may own a bucket subtree. A second one is
    refused with a Conflict condition naming the winner — two hubs on
    one prefix means that when one dies, the other takes it over.
