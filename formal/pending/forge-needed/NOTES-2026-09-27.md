Rewinds (`ForgeSyncRewind.tla`, md5 fb2389cb): the teeth and both probes
behave as expected. **ForgeSyncRewindHolds VIOLATED
Inv_ProofNeverRestsOnRetention** (242,891,987 distinct, 5 h 08 min):
`ForgeSyncRewindHolds-counterexample-2026-09-27.out`, 39 states.

The trace:
1. s1 folds p1 into f1. p1 is unnamed and RETAINED on s1's disk.
2. s1 hands off to s2.
3. The rewound p1 is re-pushed to s2, and s2's needed listing names it
   (cancelling retention on s2 only).
4. s1 claims again and restores a snapshot that names p1. Its retained list
   (from the state file) STILL holds p1, so `unlink_retained` would take a
   NAMED pack off s1's disk, and a proof over the named packs rests on it.

Proposed rule, not yet modelled: **a restore drops from `retained` every pack
the snapshot names** (a named pack is not on its way out). In code, that is
`restore.rs` when it reloads the retained list. Re-run RewindHolds with it,
and add a mutation world without it (expected: this counterexample).

Not reachable without rewinds: in `ForgeSync.tla` a pack a fold dropped is
never named again. ForgeSyncRewindStrict was deferred to give the shipped
gate the slot.
