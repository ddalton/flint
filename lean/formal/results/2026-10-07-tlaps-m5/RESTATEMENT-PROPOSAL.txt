PROPOSAL: restate Inv_NoRegress and Inv_AckedNamed (LeanP1.tla)
Written 2026-10-12 by flint-3f for flint-46, or whoever owns LeanP1.tla.
flint-46 was not running, so the proposal is left here. LeanP1.tla is NOT edited.
The candidates live in MCLeanP1M5R.tla (EXTENDS LeanP1 unchanged).
Evidence: NOTES.txt E6-E14 and RESULTS.txt.

WHY: both claims are FALSE as stated once the gateway may remove three times.
Every gate world allows at most one removal, so the gate never looked.
- Inv_NoRegress (E6): rename, re-create, delete, rename back; the tree regresses.
- Inv_AckedNamed (E8): renamed and back, deleted, a fresh install clears the
  tomb, the handle is reaped.
In every counterexample a record still names the version: an acknowledged
delete, an acknowledged rename, or a later acknowledged save. The claims look
for that record in too few places. These are scope gaps, not losses.

ALSO: Inv_AckedNamed cannot fail in a world without the retire age
(MaxAges = 0). No handle is ever reaped there, so `h \in live` names every
ack. That is every gate world, so the gate never tested the claim (E12).

PROPOSED: NR3 for Inv_NoRegress.
  Back(s, p) as now, and additionally exempt when:
  - an acknowledged delete at p retired the tree's baseline, or a version
    derived from it (udel);
  - the baseline, or a version derived from it, is acknowledged at ANOTHER
    path, i.e. it was renamed away (acked).
  It is stated as a step property over the step that takes doc[p]:
    BackR3(s, p) == /\ Back(s, p)
                    /\ ~\E d \in udel : d[1] = p /\ Derives(d[2], w[s].baseline[p])
                    /\ ~\E a \in acked : a[1] # p /\ Derives(a[2], w[s].baseline[p])
    NR3 == [][\A s \in Writers, p \in Paths :
                ~(BackR3(s, p) /\ w'[s].baseline[p] = doc[p])]_vars
  It could also stay a flag set in Consume, Sync and RPullSync, as
  `regressed` is today, with BackR3 in place of Back.

PROPOSED: AN3 for Inv_AckedNamed.
  An acked <<p, h>> is accounted for along h's whole RENAME CHAIN:
    Chain(p, h) == {p, h[1]} \cup {q : <<q, h>> \in acked}
  - the record clauses (doc, tomb, took, udel) at any path in the chain;
  - the later-ack clause at any path in the chain.
  Today the record clauses look only at p, and at the acked paths only when
  p = h[1]; the later-ack clause looks only at p.
