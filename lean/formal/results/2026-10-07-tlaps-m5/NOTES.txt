WHERE THE PLAN SAYS THE WORK IS.  Inv_AckedNamed's Sweep obligation: a
sweep deletes a live, uncited, unpreserved, non-retiring handle, so an
acked handle in that state must already be named by a non-live disjunct.
(The plan's other M5 step -- restating Derives/Supersedes over the ghost
`anc` -- was done in M0: LeanP1Anc.tla, tied by exact counts.)

E1 (MCLeanP1M5.tla: M5NoLive).  Conjecture: under Shipped, Inv_AckedNamed
holds WITHOUT its `h \in live` disjunct.  Reason: a handle enters `acked`
only through a CAS that cites it (GCas at its own path; GRename at the
destination), and each step that later uncites a cited handle leaves one
of the other names:
  GCas over it        the new save's base is the version it read (judged:
                      doc[p] = base[h']), so doc[p] derives from it (anc).
  GDelete             tomb[p] := it, and udel gains <<p, it>> (permanent:
                      udel only grows, Derives is write-once through anc,
                      Content through orig).
  GRename p -> q      it stays cited at q, and acked gains <<q, it>>.
  Install             a path of the writer's own replaces it with an upload
                      whose base chain starts at the writer's baseline; if
                      the document moved past the baseline the path is
                      contested (CommitSurfacesForeign: conflicts names
                      doc[p]); a delete sets tomb[p] or is preserved
                      (DeleteWinsPreserved).
If E1 holds, Sweep and Reap (which change only `live`) cannot break the
claim, and the obligation the plan calls the real work disappears.  The
hard cases left are the steps that REMOVE a name: GCas and Install clear
tomb[p]; a later document can replace a derived one by a non-derived one
only through the contested-path conflict, which names the replaced handle,
not its ancestors (there the later-ack disjunct, b at p with
Later(b[2], h), has to carry it); and acked copies (generation > MaxMint,
reachable only by renaming a writer-published copy) are never Later than a
fresh save's handle.

The probe M5CitedProbe (every acked handle still cited) must FAIL, so a
holding E1 is not E1 holding for the trivial reason.
