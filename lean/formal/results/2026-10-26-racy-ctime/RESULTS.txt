LeanScanRacy with the agent's clock and ctime (FINDINGS L-149), 2026-10-26

Every world before this one had the agent's `Write` stamp the current tick
and no action that set mtime: AGENTS.md's "never preserve timestamps" was
an assumption no world and no proof could see. The model gains:
  ctime          the tick of the file's last change; only the kernel sets it
                 (every Write and every SetMtime stamp it now)
  SetMtime       the agent sets mtime to any tick, past or future
                 (`touch -d`; after a Write, `cp -p` and `rsync -t`),
                 under AgentSetsMtime
  CtimeConfirm   the code: an entry whose ctime moved while size and mtime
                 did not is UNSURE, and dirty exactly when its bytes differ
  RacyOnChange   the code: the racy window is measured from the later of
                 mtime and ctime (`Later`)
  Confirm        replaces ClearRacy: an unsure entry whose hash vouched
                 records the ctime it saw and its racy flag measured now
The proof's inductive invariant moves from mtime to ctime (ReadOK, BaseOK,
GateOK, `ctime <= clock`): mtime is the agent's to set, ctime is not.
