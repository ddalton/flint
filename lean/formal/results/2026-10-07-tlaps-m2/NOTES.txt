THE CLAIMS.  Inv_CitationsLive: every cited handle is live.  Inv_OneName: no
two paths cite the same handle.  Citations change at GCas (cites gw[p]),
GRename (moves one), GDelete (drops one) and Install (cites the writer's
snap[p] for p in uploads \cap upDone \ gone, keeps or drops the rest); live
shrinks at Sweep and Reap (an uncited handle each) and, with RetireAge, not
at Collect.  So the two claims rest on: a save in flight is live and
uncited (I3); an upload at the CAS is uncited, at its own path, and -- once
verified -- live (I4, I5).

WHY THE PLAN'S I2-I5 ARE NOT ENOUGH AS STATED, and what the proof needs:
  I4 ranges over upDone.  Upload(s, p) moves p from uploads into upDone and
  must know snap[p]'s properties at that moment: uncited, unretired, in no
  save in flight, in no other tree or snapshot, at its own path.  Where do
  they come from?  THE THREAD IS `upped`: a handle that was never PUT is
  not live (live \subseteq upped), so not cited (Inv_CitationsLive), not
  retiring or aged (both \subseteq upped), not a save in flight (gw[p] is
  live); and it is PRIVATE to the writer that minted it, at its own path
  (M2Private: an un-upped tree entry is in no other tree or snapshot and
  its path component is its path) -- bytes enter a tree only by Edit (a
  fresh mint), UploadCopy (a fresh copy, PUT at once), or from the document
  (consume, sync, checkout, a widen's fetch: cited, so PUT).  So at the
  PUT, Upload's first case (h \notin upped) has everything; its second
  case mints a copy, fresh.  M2Pending carries the snapshot's privacy from
  the scan (snap := local) to the PUT, since the agent may edit the path
  meanwhile and the handle then lives in the snapshot alone.
  A first draft said "every dirty tree entry is its writer's own uncited
  handle" (out/ is the record: it never ran).  False: after Install the
  uploaded entries are CITED and still dirty until Finish rewrites the
  baseline, and a Restart in that state keeps them so -- the model's copy
  mechanism (an already-PUT handle is re-uploaded as a copy) is what keeps
  Inv_OneName, not any privacy of dirty entries.  Phrasing the invariant
  over `upped` instead of dirtiness is what made it true.
  I3 and I4 as checked exclude other trees' local AND baseline; the proof
  needs local and SNAPSHOT: a baseline never flows into a citation (Finish
  copies the writer's own snapshot into it), a snapshot does (Install cites
  it), so ~Cited is preserved across another writer's Install only if that
  writer's snapshot cannot hold the handle.  The snapshot clause follows
  inductively from the local one (a scan copies local into snap), but an
  invariant has to say it.  No conjunct mentions a baseline.
  I2e (every handle anywhere is minted) is trimmed to the snapshot: doc, gw
  and local are M0's Minted; baseline, tomb, rdoc, conflicts, acked, udel
  do not bear on these two claims.
  Inv_CitationsLive is itself a conjunct: (retiring \cup aged) \subseteq
  upped needs it (RetUpdate retires cited handles, cited => live => upped).
  M2Unverified (a scanned writer is not verified): Claim keeps `verified`,
  so M2Verified at Claim needs it.
