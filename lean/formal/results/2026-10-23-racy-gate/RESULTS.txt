LeanScanRacy with the checkout's gate (latency plan §17), 2026-10-23

The checkout measures its entries' racy flags when it opens the agent's
gate (the marker), not when it wrote each file. The model gains `gate`
(shut while the checkout runs), `Open` (writes the marker; under
MeasureAtGate it clears the flag of an entry whose mtime is a window
before the clock), and the assumption WritesWaitForGate (an agent's Write
needs the gate open). Ticks before Open are the rest of the checkout, so
a checkout that waits before its gate is the same model.
